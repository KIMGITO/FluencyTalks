# Post type ideas

**The rules that apply today are in [The contribution system](CONTRIBUTION_SYSTEM.md).** This page keeps the ideas that aren't built, and records what replaced the original plan.

## What changed from the original plan

The first version of this plan unlocked post types level by level: stories at A2, phrases at B1, lessons at B2, and special looks above that. A review on 3 November 2026 replaced it, on the principle that every learner should be able to take part from day one.

| Original plan | Now |
|---|---|
| Stories unlock at A2 | Open to every learner from day one |
| Photos unlock at A1, and images become a reward | Any member can use images |
| Text on an image unlocks at B1 | Open to everyone |
| Lessons unlock at B2 | Open to native speakers and anyone verified at B1+ |
| Help posts show Needs a fix, then Helped | One set of statuses for every post |
| Natives earn looks by correcting | Not built. Contribution shows under the level instead |

B1 still anchors the right to teach and to correct others.

## Ideas not built

| Idea | What it would be | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| Spot the mistake | A stronger writer plants errors, and readers find them | The answer key needs its own table: posts are read with `select *`, so a key on the post could be read early. Planted errors must never reach a phrasebook |
| Fill the gap | Choose or type the missing word | Same |
| Which sounds right? | Vote A or B, and natives explain | Quick to answer, so it could bring people in |
| Dialogue | Two speakers, shown as chat bubbles | A new text structure |
| Series | Linked posts, read in order | Needs a link between posts |
| Challenge | A prompt others answer with their own attempt | Replies could become practice posts |
| Rich lessons | Headers, lists and bold | A small Markdown renderer, rendered to React elements and never as raw HTML |

## What to measure

Baseline these before judging any of it. Production data wasn't available when this was written.

- **Time to first fix on a practice post**, and the share answered within 24 hours. This is the main number.
- Share of new users who post in their first week.
- Share of beginners who post again after getting a fix.
- Share of practice posts answered, per language.
- Time to reach A2 and B1.

If the public library plan ([Public content](PUBLIC_CONTENT_SEO.md)) goes ahead, its view must leave out practice posts, so learners' mistakes don't reach search engines.
